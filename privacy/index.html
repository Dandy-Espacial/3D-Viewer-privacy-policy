The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include details such as:

The Service Provider may use the collected information to send important updates, required notices, and, where permitted by law, marketing communications.

Personal Information Notice: For a better experience while using the Application, the Service Provider may require you to provide certain personally identifiable information, including but not limited to None. Any information requested will be retained and used as described in this policy.

2. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Application or its third-party software development kits (SDKs) may use cookies, SDKs, tracking pixels, and similar technologies to support internal functionality, analytics, and service delivery.

Where required by applicable law, the Service Provider will obtain your prior affirmative consent before deploying non-essential tracking technologies on your device.

3. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data held by the Service Provider. To exercise these rights, or to withdraw consent where data processing is based on consent, please contact the Service Provider directly at lambsupport@flockred.com.

4. Your California Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a resident of California, United States, you are entitled to specific statutory rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA):

  • Right to Know: The right to request disclosure of the specific categories and pieces of personal information collected about you.
  • Right to Delete: The right to request the deletion of your personal information held by the Service Provider.
  • Right to Opt-Out: The right to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: The right not to receive discriminatory treatment for exercising any of your statutory privacy rights.

To exercise your CCPA/CPRA rights, contact the Service Provider at lambsupport@flockred.com.

6. International Data Transfers

The Service Provider or its third-party service providers may store and process personal data in countries outside your country of residence, including outside the European Economic Area (EEA).

Data protection laws in other jurisdictions may differ from those applicable in your country. Where applicable law mandates safeguards for cross-border transfers, the Service Provider relies on appropriate transfer mechanisms, including:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission.
  • Adequacy decisions or other legally recognized framework mechanisms.
  • Your explicit consent, where required and legally permissible.

7. Information Disclosures

The Service Provider may disclose User-Provided and Automatically Collected Information:

  • As required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena, court order, or similar legal process.
  • When believing in good faith that disclosure is necessary to safeguard rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to an official governmental request.
  • To trusted service providers who operate strictly on our behalf, do not have an independent use of the information disclosed, and have contractually agreed to comply with the rules set forth in this privacy statement.

8. Opt-Out Rights

You can cease all collection of information by the Application immediately by uninstalling it from your mobile device. You may use the standard uninstall processes available as part of your mobile operating system or via the application marketplace.

Note: Uninstalling the Application will stop any future data collection from your device, but it does not automatically delete historical information that has previously been transmitted to the Service Provider or third-party service providers.

To request full deletion of previously transmitted personal data, withdraw consent, or exercise statutory rights, email lambsupport@flockred.com.

9. Data Retention Policy

The Service Provider retains personal data only as long as necessary for the stated operational purposes:

  • User-Provided Data: Retained for the duration of your use of the Application plus 12 months thereafter, unless a longer retention window is mandated by law.
  • Automatically Collected Data: Retained for up to 24 months from the date of collection, unless extended retention is required for legal compliance.
  • Aggregated and Anonymized Data: Retained indefinitely, as it does not personally identify you.
  • Data Required for Legal Compliance: Retained for the duration required by applicable statutory obligations.

You may request the deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting lambsupport@flockred.com.

10. Children's Privacy

The Application is not intended for children under 16 years of age, or such higher age required by applicable law in your jurisdiction. The Service Provider does not knowingly solicit data from or market the Application to children.

Where parental or guardian consent is mandated by applicable law, the Application is not intended for use without such explicit consent. If the Service Provider discovers that a child under 16 years of age has provided personal information without verified parental consent, that information will be removed from existing servers promptly.

Parents or legal guardians who become aware that their child has provided personal data should contact lambsupport@flockred.com so appropriate corrective action can be taken.
